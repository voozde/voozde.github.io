# Silly Grading System

<div style="text-align: justify;">

A couple of weeks ago I was able to start playing some videogames again.

With it, I have been motivated to go back to what I used to work on a very long time ago, a casual perspective of video-game reviews. While there may be many of them online, well, mine is not out there!

I want to use this blog / website to try and revive that kind of work / concept for a little bit of time.

Much like other websites or places, I think it's suitable to use a type of **grading system**, but rescuing some of my previous work, and due to actual numerical scores not really doing it for me anymore, I have decided to use a different one.

I've not debated it for long, but I think that for the time being I will be using this point of reference.

- Not recommended -> Due to various flaws, I'd say this is not suitable for an actual avid consumer / gamer.
- Good Experience -> Most games offer this and as such are enjoyable experiences / products.
- Quality Product -> I believe this is a product worthy of most costumer's money.
- Gaming Achievement -> Highest possible praise I can give. Please buy.

*I might change these criteria at some point, but I'd say they're pretty fair for now*

I hope you enjoy your time reading my posts.
