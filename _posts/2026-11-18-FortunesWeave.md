# Shortly on Fire Emblem Fortune's Weave

Exactly two months ago today, Fire Emblem Fortune's Weave was released on the Nintendo Switch 2.

An extremely anticipated title due to being one of Nintendo's main heavy hitters, and a direct sucessor to the Three Houses formula, both the expectations and the scope of the game were high for both fans and critics.

In this short post, I want to give a quick rambling about the game and my opinion regarding it.

Released to the massive, almost universal praise of critics, Fortune's Weave was met with several 10s across the board denoting a very high quality product.

In reality, while Fortune's Weave is abundant in quality in many areas (character designs, music, ambition, map design, unit and class design, scope, story, voice work, and a long etcetera), the actual game experience is extremely hindered by a sluggish repetition vortex that makes traversing and playing the Heroic Games more of a chore than an experience.

In my time with the game (roughly 8 chapter with Dietrich, part 1 only), the game has incentiviced me to push through the Monastery Loop thanks to what I considered at the time good writing, interesting character, and an overall good setting.

Yet, constantly trying to keep up with an "alright" idea of enjoying the game (fully looping around Dagsion -every chapter- to take the whole world experience) can only be done so many times without realizing that your last 2 hour session with the game may really have been 1:30 around menus optimizing your units (not to be confused with map preparation, that's different!) spamming meals, gifts, temple chores, *dungeon / inventory grinding*, or other well known Fire Emblem activities such as bird feeding.

Unit customization is incredibly deep in this game, but the "chore" (which I've repeated so many times both in-game and in this post) eventually makes you miss the old formula of map into map into map without so much filler in between of them. While those games have their own flaws, I may talk about them at another time, as the in-between map grind that many detractors complain about those games looks like a breeze compared to what you can do in the latest Fire Emblem installment.

Fortune's Weave review scores reminded me of a game I may talk about in the future, *Bravely Default Flying Fairy*, a game that in its original release got an extreme praise. However, as the later parts of the game unraveled, even though the narrative and the game mix well, ends up turning into a massive chore that few people will push through and be "rewarded" with what I think was, for its time, an incredible gaming experience and a unique appreciation for its world and characters

Although Fortune's Weave is an excellent game deserving of praise, but its flaws may be too glaring for some players that may eventually put the game down without experiencing all it has to offer, and this chore repetition formula may be the main point to blame. In future entries (maybe even the DLC?), perhaps Intelligent Systems may want to redesign or address this type of complain, as it has been increasingly important ever since Corrin ever got some free real state from her maid dragon back in 2015.

But with how well it has sold or been received, will they?

Thank you for reading and until next time!
